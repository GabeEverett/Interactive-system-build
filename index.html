System Brief

Live Origin Attachment

Expanding shockwaves now calculate their center coordinates relative to the cursor's live (x, y) position on every frame, eliminating the position lag and trailing delay present in early physics tests.

Persistent Interface Layer

The DOM control overlay (#info-tab) renders cleanly at a high z-index over the HTML5 canvas, making the info box visible immediately on page load without interfering with canvas rendering loops.

What feels most authentic in the behavior

Continuous Energy Echo: The stacked three-ring radial expansion attached to pointer movement gives standard cursor navigation a tactile, responsive feel without requiring continuous clicks or heavy particle physics.

Where it drifts into generic or noisy territory

Rapid Motion Clutter: Sweeping the cursor rapidly across the screen generates high-frequency ring overlaps that create visual noise.

Default Visual Tropes: The cyan-on-dark-slate palette (#45f3ff on #0b0c10) leans into generic sci-fi aesthetic defaults rather than a unique visual style.

The single specific change for v2

Velocity-Based Modulation: Map ring expansion speed and fade decay rate directly to cursor velocity (v = sqrt(Δx² + Δy²)). High-velocity sweeps will trigger thin, fast-fading shockwaves to keep the screen clean, while low-velocity movement or hovering will settle into a slow, warm ambient pulse.

Critique Diagnostic Review

Clarity: The connection between cursor movement and ripple origin is instantly legible. The info panel state clearly communicates system rules upon initial page render.

Restraint: Unnecessary visual elements (particle trails, glow filters, heavy shadows) were eliminated to focus purely on stroke width, opacity decay, and ring frequency.

Intention: The ring offset spacing (-30px, -60px) and frame-opacity fade (rgba(11, 12, 16, 0.25)) are specifically set to maintain motion trails without overwhelming the canvas.

Craft: The continuous render loop behaves deterministically across reloads, scaling smoothly to match browser resize events without distortion.
